VS

Deck B

Hostile aggro — flood the board and break their mind.

How to play (quick)
  • Each player starts at 40 sanity — it's your life and your currency.
  • Win by breaking the enemy to 0 sanity, completing 3 rituals, or decking them out.
  • Turn: Draw → Upkeep → Main → Offense → End. Use Next phase → to advance.
  • Main: click a card in hand for Play / Set trap / Ritual / Ability options.
  • Upkeep: pay each abomination or it goes feral and eats your sanity.
  • Offense: click your abominations to attack, then Done declaring; the defender clicks a blocker, then the attacker it blocks; then ⚔ Resolve combat.
  • Characters never attack but can block. At 0 personal sanity they burn out — prime ritual fuel.
  • Rituals need a ritual site (Altar/Grove) + a burned-out character sacrificed. One per turn.
  • Double-click any card image to zoom it.
