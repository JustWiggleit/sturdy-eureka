A corporate-Lovecraft card game for 2 players. Playable prototype v1.

Deck A

Ritual control — burn out your team, then take over reality.

VS

Deck B

Hostile aggro — flood the board and break their mind.

How to play (quick)
  • Each player starts at 40 sanity — it's your life and your currency.
  • Win by breaking the enemy to 0 sanity, completing 3 rituals, or decking them out.
  • Turn: Draw → Upkeep → Main → Attack → Defend → Resolve → End. Use Next phase → to advance.
  • Main: click a card in hand for Play / Set trap / Ritual / Ability options — tap the ▶ button to play it instantly, or long-press and drag it to your field.
  • Upkeep: pay each abomination or it goes feral and eats your sanity.
  • Attack: click your abominations to declare attackers (click again to stand down), then Next phase →.
  • Defend: the defender clicks one of their creatures, then the attacker it blocks, then Next phase →.
  • Resolve: hit ⚔ Resolve combat to apply damage and outcomes.
  • Characters never attack but can block. At 0 personal sanity they burn out — prime ritual fuel.
  • Rituals need a ritual site (Altar/Grove) + a burned-out character sacrificed. One per turn.
  • Double-click any card image to zoom it. Long-press and drag a hand card to your field to play it, or to the discard pile. Use the Flip button in a card's detail view to turn it over.
